## One-Sentence Mission
**AICIS transforms overwhelming global information into trusted, auditable intelligence for high-stakes decisions.**

## The Problem AICIS Solves
Not a data problem — a **decision problem**. The world has too much, conflicting, delayed, untrusted, fragmented information. Decision-makers (ministers, CEOs, military planners, emergency managers, central bankers) need to answer four questions every morning:

1. **What matters?**
2. **How serious is it?**
3. **What should we do?**
4. **Why should we trust this conclusion?**

Every feature, panel, and edge function must serve at least one of these four questions. If it doesn't, it's noise.

## Value Chain (the build hierarchy)
```
Data → Signals → Intelligence → Recommendations → Decision Support
```
Lower layers exist to feed the upper layers. We do not ship raw-data dashboards as end-products.

## Why Competitors Fall Short
- **News platforms** — say what happened, not what to do
- **Dashboards** — show metrics, not meaning
- **AI chatbots** — give answers, not proof
- **Human analysts** — slow, expensive, inconsistent, bandwidth-limited

AICIS sits *above* all of these and combines: **Intelligence + Forecasting + Recommendations + Provenance + Auditability**.

## The Three Pillars of Inevitability
What makes AICIS hard to replace is NOT AI, NOT dashboards, NOT features. It is:

### 1. Trust
Every assessment traceable to source evidence. Citations, ledger, federation signatures, provenance hashes are not features — they are the moat. Trust is the #1 problem of the AI era.

### 2. Workflow Ownership
Become the place leaders **start their day** and **make decisions from**. Bloomberg owns finance workflow; Salesforce owns sales workflow; AICIS must own the **intelligence workflow**. Minister Brief = flagship.

### 3. Network Effects Through Intelligence
Federated nodes (governments, enterprises, NGOs, universities) each contribute privacy-preserving aggregates that strengthen the shared intelligence graph. The graph compounds in value over time.

## Positioning Statement (use externally)
> "Make better decisions with intelligence you can verify."

NOT "Ask anything." NOT "We have more data." Always lead with **verifiable decisions**.

## Target Customers & Their Questions
- **Governments** — emerging risks, recommended actions, supporting evidence
- **Enterprises** — supplier risk, market changes, next moves
- **International Organizations** — crisis formation, intervention priority, evidence
- **Investors** — what's changing, why, what's next

## Practical Roadmap (anchored to this mission)
1. Finish Phase B → >95% citation coverage
2. Make Minister Brief the flagship product (decision-grade, federation-signable)
3. Build Evidence Explorer (the visible face of the trust architecture)
4. Federation-signed intelligence reports (procurement-grade exports)
5. Publish weekly public intelligence briefings (proof of value, top-of-funnel)
6. Relentlessly focus on trust, explainability, decision support — never compete as a generic AI chatbot

## Decision Filter (use on every build request)
Before shipping anything, ask:
- Does this answer one of the four questions?
- Does this strengthen Trust, Workflow Ownership, or Network Effects?
- Does this move the user further down the value chain toward a *decision*?

If no to all three — defer or cut.
