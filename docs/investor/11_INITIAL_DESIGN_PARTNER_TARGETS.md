# AICIS Initial Design-Partner Targets — Brandenburg / Berlin

## Purpose

This is a research-backed starting list for customer discovery and design-partner outreach. Inclusion does **not** imply interest, endorsement, partnership or procurement intent.

Targets are grouped by likely role:

- **P1 — potential pilot/design partner:** owns a relevant operational/resilience workflow;
- **R — research/validation partner:** may strengthen evaluation quality or applied research;
- **E — ecosystem/funding enabler:** useful for introductions, grants or company-building rather than as the first customer.

## Prioritization principles

Start with organizations where AICIS can be evaluated safely in historical replay or shadow mode and where the problem of fragmented evidence/triage is plausible from public information.

Do not send generic mass outreach. Research a specific workflow and role before contacting anyone.

## Recommended first wave

Do not contact all ten at once. First-wave order:

1. **StWB** — local, concrete infrastructure operator, strong workflow fit;
2. **Stadt Brandenburg climate/adaptation team** — local public-sector resilience fit;
3. **DABB** — potential public-sector innovation connector;
4. **E.DIS** — explicit innovation-partner posture;
5. **TH Brandenburg** — validation/research support close to the founder.

Use `06_CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md` and `07_DESIGN_PARTNER_OUTREACH.md` for every approach.

## Evidence required before changing status

Each target begins at **researched**, not "lead" or "partner".

Allowed status progression:

```text
researched
→ contacted
→ responded
→ qualified discovery
→ strong fit
→ pilot scoped
→ written design-partner intent
→ contracted pilot
→ pilot completed
→ paid continuation
```

Never skip statuses in public investor reporting. A target is not traction until the corresponding evidence exists.
