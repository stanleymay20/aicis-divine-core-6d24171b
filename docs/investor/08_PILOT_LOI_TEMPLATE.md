## 5. Baseline

Before the evaluation begins, the parties will document the existing process against which AICIS will be compared.

Possible baseline fields:

- current tools and information sources;
- current triage process;
- current median/typical time-to-triage where measurable;
- current documentation/provenance process;
- known alert/error burden;
- current decision-review roles.

No improvement claim will be made without a recorded baseline or clearly disclosed alternative evaluation design.

## 6. Evaluation metrics

The final protocol may include:

- source/provenance coverage;
- duplicate/canonicalization performance;
- precision/recall where defensible ground truth exists;
- false-positive and false-negative review;
- time-to-triage relative to baseline;
- analyst acceptance/rejection and reasons;
- decision-trace completeness;
- probabilistic calibration/Brier score where forecasts are evaluated;
- data freshness and system reliability;
- qualitative usability feedback.

The parties should define success thresholds before reviewing final results where practical.

## 7. Data, privacy and security

Before any data is transferred or connected, the parties will document:

- data owner/controller/processor roles as applicable;
- permitted data categories;
- prohibited data categories;
- retention and deletion rules;
- hosting/location constraints;
- access controls;
- audit/logging requirements;
- incident notification expectations;
- whether a separate DPA, NDA or security assessment is required.

No data access is implied by this LOI.

## 8. Human governance

AICIS outputs remain subject to human review during the pilot.

The Design Partner retains responsibility for operational decisions. Model inference, forecasts or recommendations must be distinguishable from observed evidence and source provenance.

## 9. Pilot outputs

Expected outputs may include:

- baseline report;
- evaluation protocol;
- replay/shadow-mode results;
- failure analysis;
- user feedback summary;
- security/privacy observations;
- final go/no-go report;
- mutually approved case-study summary, if any.

Neither party may publicly identify the other as a customer, partner or endorser unless separately authorized in writing.

## 10. Intellectual property

Suggested principle for legal review:

- each party retains its pre-existing IP;
- AICIS retains its platform/source/architecture IP unless otherwise agreed;
- the Design Partner retains its source data, confidential information and pre-existing materials;
- ownership/licensing of jointly created pilot-specific artifacts should be agreed before work begins;
- no transfer of IP is implied by this LOI.

## 14. Non-binding status
