## First-wave decision rule

Do not broaden outreach simply to increase volume. Prioritize learning density.

Advance a target only when at least one of the following is true:

- the current workflow has measurable manual triage or coordination burden;
- multiple data/signal sources must be reconciled before a decision;
- provenance and traceability matter to the workflow;
- missed or delayed signals have meaningful operational consequences;
- there is a safe historical-replay or shadow-mode evaluation path;
- a real internal owner is willing to define a baseline and evaluation window.

Reject or deprioritize targets where the problem is merely interesting but not painful, owned, measurable, or testable.
