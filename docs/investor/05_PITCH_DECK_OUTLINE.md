# AICIS — Evidence-Gated Pitch Deck Outline

Use this as the controlled source for a fundraising deck. Every factual claim must be backed by repository, pilot, financial or customer evidence.

## Slide 1 — AICIS

**Evidence-governed resilience intelligence for human decision-makers.**

One sentence:

> AICIS turns fragmented operational and public signals into traceable, uncertainty-aware risk analysis and decision options for resilience and continuity teams.

## Slide 2 — The problem

Show one concrete workflow:

- relevant signals arrive across disconnected systems and feeds;
- analysts manually reconcile duplicates, conflicts and context;
- provenance can be lost when information is summarized;
- action still requires accountable human judgment.

Avoid vague claims such as “the world has too much data” unless quantified and sourced.

## Slide 3 — The initial customer

**Beachhead:** municipalities, public institutions and critical-infrastructure operators with resilience / continuity responsibilities.

Show:

- user role;
- current workflow;
- buyer / budget-owner hypothesis;
- one high-value use case selected for pilot.

Mark buyer and budget assumptions as hypotheses until interviews validate them.

## Slide 4 — The product

Use the governed workflow:

```text
signals
→ canonicalize / deduplicate
→ provenance + uncertainty
→ analysis / forecast
→ decision options
→ human review
→ audit trail
```

Demonstrate the product. Do not make the slide a feature inventory.

## Slide 5 — Why it is different

AICIS separates:

1. observed evidence;
2. inferred analysis;
3. uncertainty;
4. proposed response;
5. authorized human action.

Defensibility thesis:

- evidence lineage;
- normalized cross-source data;
- evaluation history;
- institutional integrations;
- human-labelled outcomes;
- governed decision workflows.

## Slide 6 — Technical proof

Use verifiable repository evidence:

- implemented React/TypeScript product;
- PostgreSQL/Supabase architecture;
- realtime ingestion patterns;
- provenance / analytical workflows;
- RLS and privileged server boundaries;
- CI / security controls;
- scientific forecasting protocol and evaluation ledger design.

Do not convert implementation evidence into claims of real-world outcome validation.

## Slide 7 — Validation plan / traction

### Before a signed pilot

Title this slide **“Validation plan”**, not “Traction.”

Show:

- target design-partner profile;
- bounded pilot workflow;
- baseline and evaluation design;
- metrics to be measured;
- current outreach / application milestones only when verifiable.

### After real commitments

Replace with evidence such as:

- signed design partner;
- paid pilot;
- usage data;
- measured performance;
- procurement continuation;
- repeat customer.

## Slide 8 — Business model

Current hypothesis:

> paid design-partner pilot → annual enterprise subscription → expansion through integrations, data sources and workflows.

Only show exact pricing, ACV, gross margin or sales-cycle claims after evidence supports them.

## Slide 9 — Go-to-market

Sequence:

1. structured customer discovery;
2. first design partner;
3. measurable pilot;
4. paid continuation;
5. second repeatable customer;
6. expand within accounts and adjacent infrastructure domains.

Show named partners only with permission and a real relationship.

## Slide 10 — Competition

Frame alternatives honestly:

- manual analyst workflow;
- monitoring / alerting platforms;
- operational dashboards;
- risk-intelligence products;
- integration tools;
- general LLM / agent platforms;
- internal analytics teams.

The question is not whether competitors exist. The question is why AICIS wins a defined workflow.

## Slide 11 — Team

Present only verifiable founder / team facts.

Emphasize evidence of ability to build:

- data / AI engineering;
- product implementation;
- security / governance thinking;
- prior production-oriented projects where relevant.

Do not imply a larger team than exists.

## Slide 12 — Ask and milestones

Before naming a fundraising amount, build a bottom-up use-of-funds model.

The financing case should map capital to measurable milestones such as:

- company formation / legal readiness;
- first design partner;
- pilot delivery;
- evaluation report;
- security / reliability hardening;
- paid continuation;
- second repeatable customer.

## Appendix — diligence evidence

Include or link:

- system architecture;
- forecasting protocol;
- pilot blueprint;
- evaluation metrics;
- security architecture;
- data-room index;
- financial assumptions;
- incorporation / IP documents once available.

## Deck release gate

Before sending a deck, verify:

- no invented customers, pilots, revenue or team members;
- all metrics identify source and period;
- forecasts are not described as guarantees;
- implementation is not conflated with independent validation;
- partner names are used only with permission;
- every “traction” item is a verifiable commitment or measured outcome;
- the first use case is narrow enough to understand in under one minute.
