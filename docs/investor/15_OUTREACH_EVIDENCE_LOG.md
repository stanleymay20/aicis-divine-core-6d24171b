This file is the controlled record for design-partner outreach. It must distinguish preparation from actual market evidence.

## Status vocabulary

Use only the following states:

- `researched`
- `draft prepared`
- `sent`
- `reply received`
- `meeting booked`
- `discovery completed`
- `scoping proposed`
- `written pilot scope`
- `pilot active`
- `pilot completed`
- `paid continuation`
- `rejected / no fit`

Never use `customer`, `partner`, `traction`, `pipeline`, or `pilot` loosely.

## Reporting rules

### Acceptable

> Four highly relevant organizations have been approached for discovery.

Only after messages are actually sent.

> One organization agreed to a discovery meeting.

Only after explicit confirmation.

> One organization agreed to scope a design-partner pilot.

Only after explicit written evidence.

### Not acceptable without stronger evidence

- "AICIS has municipal traction."
- "StWB is an AICIS partner."
- "The city is piloting AICIS."
- "Customers are interested."
- "We have validated demand."

Investor reporting must describe the actual evidence, not the most favorable interpretation of it.
