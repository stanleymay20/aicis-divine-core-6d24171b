# AICIS — Investor One-Page Brief

## Positioning

**AICIS is an evidence-governed resilience intelligence platform that turns fragmented operational and public signals into traceable, uncertainty-aware decision support for human teams.**

AICIS is not positioned as an autonomous authority. It separates observed evidence, model inference and consequential action so that decision-makers can inspect what is known, what is inferred, what is uncertain and what requires human approval.

## Initial commercial wedge

The first market to validate is **resilience and continuity intelligence for municipalities, public institutions and critical-infrastructure operators**.

The initial buyer problem is narrow:

- relevant risk signals arrive through disconnected feeds, systems and reports;
- teams spend time reconciling sources before they can assess consequence and priority;
- provenance and uncertainty are often lost when information is summarized;
- high-consequence actions require human accountability, not opaque automation.

AICIS aims to shorten the path from fragmented signal to evidence-linked human decision.

## Product workflow

```text
Signals / feeds / open APIs / internal operational data
                    ↓
Ingestion + canonicalization + deduplication
                    ↓
Evidence provenance + relevance + uncertainty
                    ↓
Cross-domain analytical / forecasting workflows
                    ↓
Decision options + rationale + audit trail
                    ↓
Human review / approval / operational coordination
```

## What exists today

The repository demonstrates implemented product and engineering work across:

- React/TypeScript decision-support interfaces;
- Supabase/PostgreSQL data architecture;
- realtime pipelines;
- canonicalization and deduplication;
- evidence provenance;
- analytical and forecasting workflows;
- multi-tenant patterns and Row-Level Security;
- privileged server-side operations;
- human-review and governance pathways.

These are engineering capabilities, **not claims of validated operational effectiveness**.

## Proposed design-partner pilot

AICIS should enter the market through a bounded design-partner pilot with one real institution or infrastructure operator. The pilot should test one defined risk workflow, a limited set of data sources and pre-agreed success metrics.

Core evaluation dimensions:

1. signal ingestion completeness and freshness;
2. provenance coverage;
3. event/alert quality against human-labelled ground truth;
4. analyst time-to-triage versus the current workflow;
5. false-positive / false-negative behavior;
6. forecast calibration where forecasting is used;
7. human override, review and auditability;
8. reliability and security of the pilot environment.

No metric should be reported as achieved until it has been measured in a real or reproducible evaluation.

## Business-model hypothesis

The commercial model to validate is:

**paid design-partner pilot → annual enterprise subscription → expansion through additional data sources, workflows, teams and integrations.**

Pricing remains a hypothesis until buyer interviews and pilot procurement evidence establish willingness to pay.

## Why AICIS may be defensible

The intended defensibility is not a single model. It is the governed system around the models:

- evidence lineage and provenance;
- domain-specific signal normalization;
- uncertainty-aware analytical workflows;
- reusable event and forecast evaluation history;
- customer-specific operational integrations;
- human decision and audit workflows;
- accumulated institutional feedback and labelled outcomes.

The moat must be demonstrated through usage, data quality, workflow fit and measured performance rather than asserted in advance.

## Current stage

**Stage: pre-seed / technical validation.**

Current evidence supports a substantial engineering project and a credible product thesis. It does **not** yet support claims of revenue, paying customers, signed pilots, independently validated forecasting performance, national-scale deployment or production impact.

## Near-term milestones

The next investability milestones are:

1. secure one design partner;
2. select one high-value workflow and baseline process;
3. run a measurable pilot;
4. publish a reproducible evaluation report;
5. convert the successful pilot into a paid continuation or formal procurement path;
6. complete company formation and founder/IP documentation before institutional financing where required.

## Fundraising use

AICIS is suitable today for **accelerator, grant, strategic design-partner and pre-seed conversations** when presented truthfully as a technically advanced system entering commercial validation.

The fundraising story is not “AICIS is already proven at scale.” It is:

> **The core system exists. The next capital and partnerships are for focused institutional validation, productization and repeatable commercialization.**
